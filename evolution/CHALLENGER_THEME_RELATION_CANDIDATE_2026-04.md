# Challenger: Explicit Catalyst-Relation Candidate Generation

## Diagnosis

On `2026-04-21`, the queue treated `603178.SH` as a formal commercial-
aerospace candidate because it mentioned low-altitude economics, even though
its T-day reason did not contain the main candidate's specific
`商业航天+低空经济` catalyst pair. `603017.SH` did contain both terms and was
already present as a lower queue member. After the first candidate was
limit-locked, the old queue bought `603178.SH` at `+6.26%` and lost `15%`.

## Rule

Candidate generation must distinguish a concrete shared catalyst relationship
from a broad concept word. A candidate without the main path's specific
catalyst evidence remains observation-only; a candidate with the shared
`商业航天+低空经济` evidence can enter the formal queue. This is an upstream
theme/node/candidate-pool change, so the queue is regenerated before auction
facts are applied.
